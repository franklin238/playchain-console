# 60-second judge walkthrough (demo mode, no wallet needed)

Open the site (double-click `Start PlayChain.bat`, or `npm run dev` in `web/`).

1. **Home (0:00).** Point at the Spotlight hero: yesterday's most-voted game, with *Next Spotlight in …*. Hover the shelf tiles: each one takes over the hero.
2. **Connect (0:10).** *Connect wallet* → *Demo wallet*: 5,000 test $WIF appear in the top bar.
3. **Vote (0:15).** *Vote* on the hero, pick *2x*. The sheet shows the split before you confirm: half burns, 40% to the developer, 10% to the treasury. *Approve and vote*.
4. **Watch it move (0:25).** The Live Race reorders, and *Total $WIF burned* counts up.
5. **Activity (0:35).** Your vote is at the top of the feed ("just now"). Show the three leaderboards: games today, developers by earnings, voting streaks.
6. **Play (0:45).** Open Neon Tide → *Levels* → level 1. Ride two waves. Open the round PlayChain button in the corner: the game pauses and the platform menu is there.
7. **Flywheel (0:55).** About page: the five-step flywheel and the burn counter.

# Demo video script (2 minutes)

Record at 1920x1080 in a fresh browser profile with one wallet (MetaMask or Rabby) on Robinhood Chain holding some $WIF. Have the second tab open on the Blockscout page of the contract.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Black, then the PlayChain Flat boot animation | "Games onchain have no home where players decide what gets seen, and developers get paid for it. This is PlayChain Flat." |
| 0:15 | Home screen. Spotlight hero, the shelf, the Live Race | "A game console on Robinhood Chain. Yesterday's most-voted game is today's Spotlight. Below it, today's race." |
| 0:25 | Arrow keys move across the shelf, tiles lift | "It plays like a console: keyboard, gamepad or mouse." |
| 0:35 | Open Neon Tide. Ride three waves, land a Perfect, combo climbs, music opens up | "Every game runs in a sandbox with no access to your wallet. This is Neon Tide, built for the hackathon." |
| 0:50 | Quit from the pause menu. The "Did you enjoy it?" prompt slides in | "When you leave a game, you can vote for it." |
| 0:55 | Vote sheet. Pick 5x. Show the split preview: you burn, developer gets, treasury gets | "Every vote does three things: half of it burns, forty percent goes straight to the developer, ten percent funds the platform." |
| 1:05 | Approve, then Vote. Confirmed. Click the Blockscout link: three transfers, to the dead address, the developer and the treasury | "One approval for the exact amount, one vote. Here are the three transfers onchain, matching the preview exactly." |
| 1:20 | Back home. The Live Race bar for that game grows, rank changes | "The race updates live. At the end of the day the leader becomes tomorrow's Spotlight." |
| 1:35 | Developers page. Paste a meta.json URL, tile preview appears, show the listing fee | "Any developer can list a browser game. The listing fee burns in full, and from then on they earn on every vote." |
| 1:50 | Activity page: total $WIF burned, total paid to developers, the feed with tx links | "Everything is public: what's been burned, what developers have earned, every transaction." |
| 1:58 | The flywheel diagram from About, then the contract address | "More games, more votes, more burn, more income. PlayChain Flat. Live on Robinhood Chain." |

Show the flywheel diagram on screen for at least 3 seconds; judges score the flywheel directly.

Tips:
- Keep "Reduce motion" off and sound on. Neon Tide's music reacting to the dive is a strong 10 seconds.
- Hide bookmarks and extensions. Zoom the browser to 110% so text reads on a phone.
- Never show a seed phrase or private key on screen.
