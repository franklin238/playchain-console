**What it does**
PlayChain Flat is a console-style home screen for web3 games on Robinhood Chain. Players browse a shelf of games and play them right in the console, each one sandboxed with no access to their wallet. When they like a game, they vote for it with $WIF. Every vote is split onchain in one transaction: 50% is burned, 40% goes straight to the game's developer and 10% funds the platform. Developers list a game by burning a 1,000 $WIF fee, then earn from every vote it gets. The most-voted game of each day becomes the next day's Spotlight, the hero of the home screen, and a new race starts. The shelf launches with four games, three of them built during the hackathon: Neon Tide (a rhythm racer on a living neon ocean), Hollow Kingdom (rebuild a castle by day, defend it by night) and Signal Lost (route a fading signal through a dead network).

**How it uses $WIF**
Listing fees burn in full. Votes burn half, pay developers 40% and the treasury 10%, straight from the voter's wallet. A developer voting for their own game burns their share too, so the Spotlight can't be bought at a discount. The contract never holds $WIF and its owner can't move anyone's tokens.

**The flywheel**
Developers burn $WIF to list. Players vote with $WIF, half of it burns. Developers earn 40% of every vote, so they bring their players to vote for them and win the Spotlight. A new Spotlight every day gives everyone a reason to come back. More games bring more players, more votes, more burn and more developer income.
