HOW TO PLAY

Land matching the slope: PERFECT within 10°, GOOD within 25°

RIDING THE WAVES

  • Hold SPACE / W / ↑, press and hold the screen, or hold (A) to dive. Diving down the face of a wave builds speed.
  • Release as you reach the crest to launch. Holding in the air pulls you down faster; letting go lets you glide.
  • Steer with A / D / ← →, by dragging, or with the left stick.

LANDINGS AND COMBO

  • PERFECT: land within 10° of the slope. +500 × combo and the combo grows (up to x10).
  • GOOD: within 25°. +200 × combo.
  • SPLASH: a steeper landing, or hitting a buoy, rock or lightning. You lose speed and your combo resets.
  • Every metre also scores × your combo, so keeping it alive matters most.

PICKUPS

  • Blue orbs (+50): each one adds half a second of boost fuel, up to 8 seconds.
  • Boost: press SHIFT, (B) or tap with a second finger. It burns fuel until it runs out; press again to stop. The bar under BOOST shows your fuel.
  • Magenta gates (+300): fly through them.
  • White pearls (+2,500): they float high, so you need a strong launch.

HAZARDS

  • Orange buoys and bigger grey rocks: steer around them. Passing close scores a near miss (+150).
  • Whirlpools (choppy water) drag you sideways and slow you down. Jump over them.
  • Lightning (surge): a marked spot flashes before it strikes. Be somewhere else.
  • The storm follows you. The bar at the top shows how close it is. If it catches you, the run ends.

LEVELS AND STARS

12 levels, each adding one new thing. Reach the finish for ★, beat the score target for ★★, and the higher target for ★★★. Finishing a level unlocks the next. Endless Tide and the Daily Tide have no finish line: ride until the storm catches you.
